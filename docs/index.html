Privacy Policy for Curious Road

Last updated: July 23, 2024

Introduction

Curious Road ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application "Curious Road" (the "App").

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Location Data: We collect your precise location information to provide location-based audio stories and notifications about nearby points of interest.
  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers, operating system version, and app usage statistics.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with the App, including which stories you listen to and your preferences.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Location Services: When you enable location services, we collect your GPS coordinates to provide relevant content.
  • App Analytics: We collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • Crash Reports: We may collect crash reports to identify and fix technical issues.

How We Use Your Information

Core App Functionality

  • Location-Based Services: To provide audio stories about nearby points of interest
  • Notifications: To send you proximity alerts when approaching story locations
  • Audio Playback: To play location-specific audio content
  • App Performance: To optimize the App's functionality and user experience

Analytics and Improvement

  • Usage Analytics: To understand how users interact with the App
  • Performance Monitoring: To identify and resolve technical issues
  • Feature Development: To improve existing features and develop new ones

Data Storage and Security

Data Storage

  • Local Storage: Some data is stored locally on your device for offline functionality
  • Cloud Storage: Audio files and waypoint data are stored securely in Firebase Cloud Storage
  • Database: Waypoint information is stored in Firebase Firestore

Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted
  • Access Controls: We implement strict access controls to protect your information
  • Regular Updates: We regularly update our security measures to protect against threats

Third-Party Services

Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase services for:

  • Firestore Database: Storing waypoint and story data
  • Cloud Storage: Storing audio files
  • Analytics: Understanding app usage patterns

Google Maps

We use Google Maps API for:

  • Interactive Maps: Displaying your location and nearby points of interest
  • Geocoding: Converting coordinates to readable addresses
Data Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except as described in this policy.

Your Rights and Choices

Location Permissions

  • Foreground Location: Required for basic app functionality
  • Background Location: Required for proximity notifications and geofencing
  • Control: You can disable location services in your device settings

Data Access and Deletion

  • Access: You can request access to your personal data
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data
  • Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate data

Opt-Out Options

  • Notifications: You can disable push notifications in the App settings
  • Analytics: You can opt out of analytics data collection
  • Location: You can disable location services at any time

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

International Users

If you are using our App from outside the United States, please note that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States where our servers are located.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Sending you a notification if required by law

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Union, our legal basis for processing your personal data includes:

  • Consent: When you agree to our data collection
  • Legitimate Interest: To provide and improve our services
  • Contract Performance: To fulfill our obligations to you

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect
  • Know whether we sell or disclose your personal information
  • Say no to the sale of your personal information
  • Access your personal information
  • Equal service and price

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

Typically, we retain data for:

  • Location Data: Until you disable location services
  • Usage Analytics: 2 years
  • Crash Reports: 1 year
  • Account Data: Until you delete your account
